🔍 Key Empirical Findings

1. Category A Price Inversion & All-Time High in 2026

Category A reached an unprecedented historical peak of 39,001 in November 2026 (Exercise 1), exceeding Category B (32,334) due to high EV mainstream adoption and concentrated middle-market demand (1.70x bid-to-quota ratio).

2. The October 2023 Structural Squeeze (50k+ Barrier)

Supply restriction throttled Category B quota down to 472 units, generating the record all-time peak of 50,001. Category E surged to 58,004 with an intense supply-price negative correlation of r = -0.768.

3. Premium Spread Compression (2023 vs 2026)

The Cat B luxury premium over Cat A collapsed from an annual average spread of +7,263 in 2023 down to just +,560 in 2026, signaling structural structural price inelasticity across car classifications.

🎯 Strategic Recommendations for Dealerships & Fleets

Recommendation 1: Dynamic Bidding Tranches for Dealerships

Automotive dealerships should transition away from fixed retail markup and deploy dynamic COE pass-through hedging. When bid-to-quota ratios surpass 1.5x in Exercise 1 of any quota window, lock forward orders using Cat E transferability to mitigate margin erosion.

Recommendation 2: EV Fleet Leasing Over Direct Capital Ownership

Corporate fleet operators should shift toward 5-year operating leases rather than 10-year outright vehicle purchases. With annual COE depreciation topping 3,900/year per mass-market vehicle, leasing shelters balance sheets from end-of-cycle quota quota supply ramp-up risks.
